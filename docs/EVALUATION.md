How these labels were made:

- **Who.** Answers are anonymous, and nobody's identity was checked. The builder reports that all of them are human responses from people other than the builder, more than one person, and that the builder answered none directly.
- **A guided set.** The builder reports guiding a friend through one entire set of 20, so that set isn't independent of the builder. Which code it is isn't recorded, so it can't be left out. Removing any one code with exactly 20 answers would leave 84, 84, 84 drafts labelled.
- **Timing.** 3 of the codes (60 answers) took a median of about 1.7 seconds a draft, nearly the same time on every draft, including one with 9 rules ticked. That pace doesn't fit reading and ticking live on the page, and how those answers were entered isn't known. They are counted here because the builder reports them as human, so read the scores with that in mind.

Agreement between people (Cohen's kappa): 0.455 over 361 paired decisions. Agreement with the builder's labels: 0.724. The builder settles no disagreement: 23 of 1976 rule decisions were left out because people split evenly.

## Fix acceptance rate

Measured inside the app, per rule, on Guideline Health: accepted or lightly edited (30% or less changed) over everything decided, with the last decision on a finding winning, so an Undo counts. Seeded history and automated browsers are excluded. It lives in each browser's workspace, so a number only exists where people have used the app; any figure reported for this project says how many decisions it comes from and whose they were.
